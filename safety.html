⚠️ Welding without proper safety precautions can cause serious injury or death.

Welding Safety Guidelines

Welding involves high temperatures, bright arcs, and hazardous materials. Following proper safety procedures reduces risks and ensures a safe working environment.

Protective Gear

Wear a helmet, gloves, flame-resistant clothing, and boots.

Eye Protection

Use proper shading to prevent UV and infrared damage.

Ventilation

Work in ventilated areas to avoid harmful fumes.

Fire Prevention

Keep flammable materials away and have an extinguisher ready.

Equipment Check

Inspect tools, cables, and machines before use.

Electrical Safety

Avoid live wires and keep equipment dry.

Proper Training

Only weld if trained to reduce accidents.

Work Area Safety

Keep your workspace clean and organized.
