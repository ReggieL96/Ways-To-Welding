Welding Safety

Welding can be dangerous if proper precautions are not taken. It involves intense heat, bright light, sparks, and harmful fumes. Following basic safety guidelines helps prevent injuries and keeps you safe while working.

Protective Gear

Always wear a welding helmet, gloves, flame-resistant clothing, and safety boots to protect against heat and sparks.

Eye Protection

Use a proper welding helmet with the correct shade to protect your eyes from harmful UV and infrared radiation.

Ventilation

Work in a well-ventilated area or use exhaust systems to avoid breathing in dangerous fumes and gases.

Fire Prevention

Keep flammable materials away from your workspace and always have a fire extinguisher nearby.

Equipment Check

Inspect your tools and cables before use to ensure everything is working properly and safely.

Welding Safety Guidelines

Safety is paramount when working with welding equipment. Always follow these guidelines to protect yourself and others.
