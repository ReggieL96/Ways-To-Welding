⚠️ Welding without proper safety precautions can cause serious injury or death.

Welding Safety Guidelines

Welding involves high temperatures, bright arcs, and hazardous materials. Following proper safety procedures reduces risks and ensures a safe working environment.

Protective Gear

Wear a welding helmet, gloves, flame-resistant clothing, and steel-toe boots.

Eye Protection

Use proper helmet shading to prevent eye damage from UV and infrared rays.

Ventilation

Work in ventilated areas to avoid inhaling harmful fumes and gases.

Fire Prevention

Keep flammable materials away and always have a fire extinguisher nearby.

Equipment Check

Inspect tools, cables, and machines before welding.

Electrical Safety

Avoid contact with live wires and keep equipment dry.

Proper Training

Only weld if trained. Knowledge reduces accidents and improves quality.

Work Area Safety

Keep your workspace clean, organized, and well-lit.
