Welcome To The World Of Welding

Welding is a powerful skill used to join metals together using heat, pressure, or electricity.

This website will teach you about welding techniques, equipment, safety practices, and how welding impacts the modern world.

What Is Welding?

Welding is a process that permanently joins two or more pieces of metal.

Different welding methods are used depending on the material and project.

Common Welding Processes

MIG Welding

Beginner-friendly and widely used.

TIG Welding

High precision and clean welds.

Stick Welding

Strong welds for outdoor work.

Tools Used In Welding

  • Welding Machine: Power source
  • Helmet: Eye protection
  • Gloves: Heat protection
  • Grinder: Metal prep
  • Clamps: Hold pieces

Careers In Welding

Welders work in construction, aerospace, and manufacturing.

Many also use robotic and advanced welding systems.
