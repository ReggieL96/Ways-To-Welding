Welding Safety

Welding can be dangerous if proper precautions are not taken. It involves intense heat, bright light, sparks, and harmful fumes. Following basic safety guidelines helps prevent injuries and keeps you safe.

Protective Gear

Wear a helmet, gloves, flame-resistant clothing, and safety boots.

Eye Protection

Use the correct helmet shade to prevent UV and infrared damage.

Ventilation

Always weld in a well-ventilated area or use exhaust systems.

Fire Prevention

Keep flammable items away and have a fire extinguisher nearby.

Equipment Check

Inspect tools and cables before starting work.
