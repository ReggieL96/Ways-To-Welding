What is Welding?

Welding is a fabrication process used to join materials, typically metals or thermoplastics, by applying heat, pressure, or both. This creates a strong and permanent bond.

It is widely used in industries such as construction, automotive manufacturing, aerospace, and shipbuilding because of its strength and reliability.

Common types include MIG, TIG, and Stick welding, each suited for different materials and applications.

Because welding involves intense heat and harmful fumes, proper safety equipment like helmets, gloves, and protective clothing is essential.
