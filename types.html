Welding Types

TIG Welding

Precise welding using a tungsten electrode and shielding gas.

MIG Welding

Fast and beginner-friendly welding using a wire feed.

Stick Welding

Durable and portable welding method using flux-coated rods.

Flux-Cored Welding

Wire-fed welding with built-in flux protection.
